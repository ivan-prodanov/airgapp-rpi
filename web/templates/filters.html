Filters

Each filter is a curated list of allow-listed domains. Toggle one on to apply it to every Filtered device. System filters ship with the binary and cannot be deleted (their domain list is still editable). The Tesla AP/Nav filter is what keeps Autopilot maps working without letting the car talk to Hermes.
