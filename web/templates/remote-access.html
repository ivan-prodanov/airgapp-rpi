Remote access

The Pi sits behind carrier-grade NAT — there's no public IP to point a phone at. Tailscale gives every device a stable .ts.net hostname that works from anywhere. Encrypted (WireGuard), peer-to-peer when possible, free for personal use. Your phone needs the Tailscale app on the same tailnet; reach /garage from there.

Phone setup
One-time, ~60s
  1. Install the Tailscale app from the App Store or Play Store.
  2. Sign in with the same account that owns the tailnet (where you generated the key).
  3. Open https://:8443 in Safari/Chrome. First visit warns about the self-signed certificate — accept it once, or download netfilter-ca.crt and install it on your phone as a trusted root.
